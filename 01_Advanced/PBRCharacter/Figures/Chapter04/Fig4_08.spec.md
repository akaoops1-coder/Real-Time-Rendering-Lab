# Fig4_08 — Piecewise Source Radius

## Purpose
하나의 Destination radius r가 Pupil / Iris Ring / Outer의 세 구간에서 SourceRadius로 변환됨을 보여준다. 실제 Render가 아닌 Concept / Arithmetic Example이다.

## Inputs
P=.055, I=.16, PupilScale=1.2 → p=.066, IrisScale=1.1 → i=.176, OuterTransition end O=.30. Fig4_07과 달리 IrisScale=1.1을 사용하여 Outer 보정이 보이도록 했다. Gray dashed line is identity. Colored polyline is the ideal piecewise expression away from EqualsThreshold and guard activation.

## Observed Formula
- Pupil: r*(P/p). Normalized progress tPupil=r/p, equivalent to Lerp(0,P,tPupil).
- Iris: Lerp(P,I,Saturate((r-p)/Max(i-p,.0001))).
- Outer: r+(I-i)*(1-Saturate((r-i)/Max(.30-i,.0001))).
- Boundary p→P, i→I, O→O; scale1/1 yields ideal rho=r because P=p, I=i and outer correction zero.

## Actual / Proposed Distinction
Audited graph has Max(.0001) on Iris and Outer denominators and Delta/Max(r,.0001), but no explicit Max on pupil denominator p and no radius order clamp. Figure does not claim these protections are implemented. If EqualsThreshold=1e-5 and central guard effects remain, exact identity is not proven near center/boundaries. C0 continuity of ideal formula does not imply derivative continuity, filtering stability, physical refraction, or rendered validation.
